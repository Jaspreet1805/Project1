Important PC Components

A computer is made up of several components that work together. Understanding what each component does is one of the first steps toward building your computer.

CPU

The Central Processing Unit, or CPU, performs the calculations and instructions needed for the computer to operate, often being described as the brain of the computer.

When choosing a CPU, consider its performance, price, and compatibility with the motherboard.

GPU

The Graphics Processing Unit, or GPU, is responsible for processing graphics and displaying images on the monitor.

A powerful GPU is especially useful for gaming, video editing, 3D modeling, and other graphics-intensive tasks.

Motherboard

The motherboard is the main circuit board inside the computer. Components such as the CPU, RAM, storage, and graphics card connect to the motherboard.

The motherboard must be compatible with your CPU and should provide the features needed for the rest of your build.

RAM

Random Access Memory, or RAM, temporarily stores information that programs are actively using. Having enough RAM helps the computer run multiple programs efficiently.

Storage

Storage holds your operating system, applications, games, documents, photos, and all other files.

Solid-state drives, or SSDs, are commonly used because they provide fast startup and loading times.

Power Supply

The Power Supply Unit, or PSU, supplies electrical power to the computer's components.

A power supply should be reliable and provide enough power for the entire system.

Computer Case

The computer case holds and protects all of the components. The case must have enough room for the motherboard, graphics card, power supply, cooling system, and other hardware.

Cooling

Computers generate heat while operating. Fans and CPU coolers help remove this heat and keep the components at safe operating temperatures.
